1: Understand the Problem

University students consistently struggle to connect their day-to-day spending 
decisions to their overall financial health. While most students have access to bank 
transaction data, they lack the tools to transform that raw data into meaningful patterns 
— leaving them unable to identify where their money actually goes, why it disappears 
faster than expected, and whether they are building any financial resilience at all.
